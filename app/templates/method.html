Pipeline

Each step runs on its own, or all together with python -m pipeline.run

  1. 1Extract

    Luxembourg funds, their dated name changes and their manager and umbrella links, from GLEIF.

    extract/gleif_extract.py
  2. 2Load

    CSV files into DuckDB, each load logged with its SHA-256 fingerprint.

    pipeline/load_raw.py
  3. 3Transform and test

    dbt models: cleaning, formatting-only changes removed, name history, ESG vocabulary, marts.

    transform/
  4. 4AI review

    Claude re-reads ESG renames the keyword rules found and corrects their type, with a reason.

    ai/classify_names.py
  5. 5Evaluate

    Rules and AI are scored against renames labelled by hand.

    ai/evaluate.py
